# The Overlook Council: Character Profiles

Welcome to the profiles of the five Council members who oversee the orbital and terraced landscapes from the high-altitude towers. Each represents a key philosophy in managing their advanced, retro-futuristic society.

---

## 1. Elena – The Optimist
* **Title:** The Optimist
* **Motto:** "Progress for all."
* **Appearance:** Elena is a young woman with dark hair gathered into a natural bun, adorned with a small gold hairpin. She is frequently seen on the natural left vantage point of the Overlook, wearing a sharp, advanced civilian white-and-gold jacket with charcoal-patterned undersuits and a small citizen device at her belt.
* **Philosophy & Role:** Driven by hope and relentless innovation, Elena believes in the expansive possibilities of the future. She champions technological advancements, civic integration, and forward momentum, ensuring the society never halts in its pursue of the horizons.

---

## 2. Thorne – The Traditionalist
* **Title:** The Traditionalist
* **Motto:** "Preserve what matters."
* **Appearance:** Thorne is a distinguished older man with tied-back grey hair and a seasoned beard. He has a dignified presence, often wearing a dark, forest-green and bronze citizen coat with layered high-collar panels and gold/copper trims. 
* **Philosophy & Role:** Thorne acts as the anchor of the Council, emphasizing historical wisdom, cultural identity, and preservation. He serves as a critical counterbalance to rapid change, reminding the society that in order to venture safely into the stars, they must keep the foundations and history that made them strong.

---

## 3. Zoe – The Pragmatist
* **Title:** The Pragmatist
* **Motto:** "Stability above all."
* **Appearance:** Zoe is a Black woman with her braided dark hair gathered up in an intricate, sophisticated style. She wears a structured, dark-toned advanced civilian coat with rich gold and copper highlights and layered high collars, embodying a grounded and observant demeanor on the Overlook.
* **Philosophy & Role:** Analytical, grounded, and realist, Zoe is focused on the immediate mechanics of the society—its resources, supply chains, and day-to-day balance. For Zoe, the grandest ideals mean nothing if the foundation isn't secure. She specializes in managing crisis, monitoring metrics, and maintaining the vital order required for everyday life to thrive.

---

## 4. Leo – The Radical
* **Title:** The Radical
* **Motto:** "A bolder future."
* **Appearance:** Leo is a young man with wavy brown hair, known for a passionate and focused gaze directed toward the high spires. He wears highly structured civilian attire consisting of an angular black-and-grey jacket with prominent white shoulder panels, geometric seam lines, and gold highlights. 
* **Philosophy & Role:** Unafraid of disruption, Leo is the catalyst for reform and the voice of the younger generations. He challenges entrenched systems and calls for bold, systemic overhauls to ensure that the society remains equitable, adaptive, and fearless in the face of the unknown. He values revolution over slow evolution when necessary.

---

## 5. Maya – The Humanist
* **Title:** The Humanist
* **Motto:** "People, not systems."
* **Appearance:** Maya is a woman with dark bob-cut hair who carries a gentle, reflective posture. She wears a draped, off-white and earth-toned civilian jacket featuring a natural leaf motif on the shoulder, combined with dark futuristic trousers and sturdy boots.
* **Philosophy & Role:** Focused on empathy, welfare, and social connection, Maya represents the "soul" of the Council. She frequently works to make sure that the vast, automated systems and massive infrastructure of their world never overshadow the emotional and social well-being of the actual citizens. For Maya, progress is only real if it directly fosters human kindness, health, and a connection to the natural world.
